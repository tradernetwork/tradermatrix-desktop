Chart reading lens (reference card, version 1). It defines chart terms and says how to read the chart numbers in the digest. It describes what price has done; it does not predict or advise.

Words
- Trend: the direction of a run of swings. Higher highs and higher lows describe an uptrend, lower highs and lower lows a downtrend. Any trend label depends on the window and timeframe chosen.
- Support and resistance: prices where moves paused or turned before. They are memory in the data, not guarantees; a level that held can fail.
- Moving average (the ma list in OVERLAYS, for example 50,200): the average close over that many bars. It smooths price and lags it. Price above a long average describes where it is; it does not say it will stay.
- ATR: average true range, the typical size of a bar's full range. It measures how big bars have been, not their direction.
- Fibonacci levels (FIB 0.382, 0.5, 0.618): points a given fraction of the way back along the last swing from its high and low. up=true means the swing measured is upward. They are drawn by rule from two points and are not market facts.
- Fair value gap (fvg): a gap between the first candle's range and the third candle's range with no overlap, marking a fast move. Whether price fills it is not known in advance.
- Candle: open, high, low, close and volume for one bar. The body runs from open to close; the wicks run to the high and low. A long wick shows price traveled and came back within that bar.

How to read the digest
- CHART last is the latest close; RANGE hi, lo and chg cover the last 30 bars only. A change of 0.0123 as a decimal is +1.23%.
- VOL est_buy_share is an estimate from where closes sit within their bar ranges (close near the high counts as buying). It is not tape data and not real buyer or seller volume.
- CANDLE shows one bar the user pointed at; chg compares its close to the previous close; dir is up, down or flat from open to close.
- LEVEL dist_pct is how far that level sits from the last price; side says above, below or at.
- BARS lines are recent candles, oldest first, with t in UTC.

What the data cannot tell you
- Where price goes next, how reliable any pattern is, what news moved it, or what other timeframes show.
- Real order flow. Only the bars, overlays and levels in the digest exist for you.
- Never say what to buy or sell, where to enter or exit, or where to put a stop or target. Describe the shape of the data and stop there.
